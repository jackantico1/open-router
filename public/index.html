Settings

Temperature controls how predictable the wording is. Precise sticks to the most likely words, so answers are focused and consistent: good for facts, code, and data. Balanced suits everyday questions and writing. Creative takes more chances, which helps brainstorming and fiction but can drift off-topic. It doesn't make a model smarter or more accurate. Models that don't support a setting ignore it. Reasoning models often ignore temperature.
